Repair-first approach

Diagnose before buying parts. Start with visual checks and simple tests, identify the smallest failed component, then repair or adjust it where appropriate. Replace a complete assembly only when testing or the correct service procedure justifies it.

Compatibility note: This guide currently assumes a carburetted Thunderbird 350 Twin Spark, possibly around 2017. Confirm the exact year and variant against the registration certificate and the correct Royal Enfield parts catalogue before ordering anything. No unverified OEM part numbers or prices are shown.
